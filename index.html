Questions

What computer do I need?

Scripts, voices and stock-footage videos run on any recent Windows or Mac computer. Making video on your own GPU needs an NVIDIA card with 8 GB or more: expect minutes per shot on 8 GB, much less on 12 GB and up. Without one, use stock footage and your own keys.

Do I need API keys?

No. Plotweave works with local models and stock footage alone. Keys are optional, for premium models on the shots that matter most.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Plotweave adds no watermark and claims no rights. Check the licence of any model or stock clip you use; each video comes with a credits file.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once your local models are installed. Cloud models and stock search need a connection.

What comes with the source code?

The full codebase, architecture docs and an extension guide, so you can add models, change the interface, or build your own product on it.
